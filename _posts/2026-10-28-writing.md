title:  "writing - a poem"

sometimes, i like to scribble without any choices, without the feeling of words on the page that don't look quite right, sentences that bend the wrong way and force shapes that don't feel right on my tongue as i read my words out loud. i don't have to worry about my spelling or grammar, or any of those meddling things teachers teach that stop the words from running out my mouth like a river. all i see are the words running from my brain to my hands to the page bleeding out of me like a cut that will never scab over. this is the feeling of writing that i love so much. the feeling of me and nothing else. my page is not constrained because my _mind_ is not caged by the rules of what my art should or shouldn't be. they call it stream-of-consciousness, just another rule and another box interrupting my narrative. a band-aid on my cut that needs to bleed, needs to bleed out all the wrongs and rights and rules that govern my existence, those metal bars that trap my art in a cage when it was meant to fly.

_fly_

my writing is a bird and a river and a stream and the universe all at once, held back by nothing but my own fickle mind. my heart swells with emotion, emotion none of you will ever see, none of you will ever read, none of you will ever feel, because i'm writing with invisible ink, writing for the sake of writing and not for anyone else's eyes, unless of course, i, by some miracle decide this stream of _my_ consciousness is talented enough to share with you. 

there's another thought. what is talent really when it comes to art? is my writing only talented if it meets a criteria or fulfills a rubric? when i write on this page with invisible ink, does it show my talent? does it demonstrate that i am worthy of your praise, or that i was born with some innate ability to create that holds me above you? what is the standard i am striving to meet and does it exist? if it exists, did i create it, or am i simply abiding by it? are we all fools tricked by the human's need to measure, to grade, to judge, to assess. if this supposed standard does not exist, why do i imagine it so? why have we together created this imagined prison for ourselves and our reality? 

we are so drawn to rules and rubrics and criteria that we have forgotten what makes us _us_. we write and we draw and we think and we sing to skies even when nobody is out there listening. we pour our minds and our hearts into molding these beautiful shapes, perfectly cut each time because there truly is no standard of perfect. my words bleed from me like a cut and my heart cries out to you like a scream, not a scream of joy or suffering or emotion, but a scream of a deeper desire. 

because i do think that our need to create, our unbridled desire to leave behind those little trails of ourselves on this shared world, on this shared existence, that drive to make art and sing and scream to the skies, it comes from somewhere deep, deep, inside us, buried away in the pockets of our minds the neuroscientists have never touched but the philosophers have. my art, which i have written in invisible ink, is here not for you to see, but for me to feel.

i am short-lived on this world, this world that has exploded and imploded and been a trillion different things all at once. compared to its billions and billions of years of wisdom, its teachings as old as time itself, i am hit with the realization that my fifteen years is nothing. a speck, a dot, a drop in the much larger ocean. this is me in all my glory, and yet. and yet i create. alas, that is and always will be the way of the foolish human, the creature gifted with this thing called life and a never-ending need to live it to the fullest.

i am scribbling on this page in invisible ink not because i want it to disappear, but because i want to unclasp and free that pocket of me that makes things just to make them, that sings simply to sing, that bleeds just to feel the sting for a moment and to watch the blood run onto the page, creating yet another artwork. my words pour out of me like a raging river of thought, running though you and me, dear reader, this is my gift to you. for just a moment, you can watch my ink on the page and watch it disappear.

it's almost cathartic to watch my words and my art disappear just like i will one day. maybe it's some sick, ugly part of me that wants to see my words die because i will die, but is it so sick and ugly to want to make my art just like me? to make the true manifestation of myself on this universe, my handprint on the cave walls, fade away because i will fade away? perhaps it is some primal masochism, something _human_ like me, that wants to see my beautiful lines in the sand washed away by the sea. 

well.
perhaps not washed _away_. you, of course, with your wonderful consciousness will be here to witness my ink on the page. you will remember, perhaps, the salty wind running through your hair as i carve my words into the shore with futility. maybe you will remember the way my hands began to bleed by the third paragraph. maybe you will remember the wind and the sand and me and the ocean, but not the words themselves. and perhaps that itself is a pretty thing indeed. it is a pretty, pretty thing that you were here, whoever you are, and that was enough. 

and, though this gives me some sadness for reasons i can't right now ascertain, maybe i and only i will bear witness to these words and bear witness to my art. maybe i will never show this page with invisible ink on it in time for anyone to see the words, and maybe you were never even there on the beach watching me. 

maybe one day i will think about this page, and i will smile, but i will not remember how i wrote, in length, about my joy, my blood, the life-breath that i give this page and my art, the creation i'm creating simply because i feel the need to do so, these are things i will forget, but maybe it's also a pretty thing to forget. maybe it's a pretty thing for nobody at all to remember i was ever here because _i_ remembered i was here. 

and that is also enough.

because
because this is enough. this bird staring up at me, _my_ bird. these scribbles on the beach and the blood on my hands and the river gushing by and the universe and my person. 

this is enough.
