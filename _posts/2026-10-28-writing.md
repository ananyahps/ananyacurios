The other day, I saw women arguing for the repealing of the 19th amendment. 

...in case you need a reminder, the nineteenth amendement is the amendment affirming women's suffrage.

I shouldn't have to explain why that statement is deeply unsettling in the big 2026, especially before the midterm elections. In all honestly, we shouldn't even have had to fight for the right to vote at all. 

I think this is a slippery slope towards more of our rights taken away. It begins with suffrage, and it ends in _The Handmaiden's Tale_. A spectacular book, by the way.

That's all. Just wanted to put it out there.
