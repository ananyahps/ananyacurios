our minds are funny things…painting rainbows right across the sky and tinting the world bright pink only to shatter the glasses right into our eyes when we wake up…see the expanse for what it is…mottled colors in distorted shapes…nothing sitting quite right and everything sitting just how it was meant to be…funny things, our minds…always, forever, thinking, thinking.
